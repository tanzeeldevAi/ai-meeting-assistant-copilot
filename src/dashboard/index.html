Connect the AI that writes your answers Sign in once with the subscription you already have. Your browser opens, and this page carries on by itself.

Everything here does two jobs: it tells the AI what the meeting is about, and the names and jargon get fed into the speech recogniser so it stops mishearing them.

This works like a script. If you wrote a line for it, it says your line — close to word for word, only adjusted to fit the moment. If they ask something you did not prepare for, it still answers, but only using the facts, prices and claims that appear here, in your voice. It will not invent a service or a promise you never wrote.
Anything marked DON'T SAY or NOTE stays private — it reads those to decide what to say, and never says them out loud.
